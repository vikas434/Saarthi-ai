# Bluepine Books (sample product — replace with yours in the Setup tab)

Bluepine Books is online accounting software for US small businesses and freelancers, from the fictional company Bluepine Inc.

## Plans (billed monthly, prices in US dollars)
- Simple Start: $30 per month. Automatic bank connections, invoicing, expense tracking, and tax-time reports for your CPA. One user.
- Essentials: $60 per month. Everything in Simple Start, plus bill pay, time tracking, and 3 users.
- Plus: $90 per month. Everything in Essentials, plus inventory, project tracking, and 5 users.
- Payroll add-on: $50 per month plus $6 per employee. Runs payroll and pays payroll taxes.
- Live Bookkeeper add-on: $200 per month. A named bookkeeper reviews your books every month and answers questions within one business day.

## Facts
- 30-day free trial on every plan. No credit card needed to start.
- Cancel any time. Monthly billing. Annual billing gives 2 months free.
- Data import from spreadsheets (Excel or Google Sheets) and other accounting software is included and takes about 2 business days.
- Bank connections work with 12,000+ US banks and credit unions. Cash transactions must be entered by hand.
- Support: chat 6am to 6pm Pacific, Monday to Friday. Phone support only on Plus.
- Refund: full refund within 30 days of the first payment. No refunds after that.
- Card payments on invoices cost 2.9% plus 25 cents per payment. Bank transfers (ACH) cost 1%.
- Bluepine Books does not file taxes for the customer. Reports are ready to hand to their CPA.

## Never promise
- Guaranteed tax savings, same-day data import, or a discount beyond the annual plan.
