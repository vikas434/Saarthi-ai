# LedgerLeaf (sample product — replace with yours in the Setup tab)

LedgerLeaf is an online bookkeeping service for small businesses and freelancers.

## Plans
- Starter: Rs 999 per month. Automatic bank feeds, invoicing, GST-ready reports. One user.
- Growth: Rs 2,499 per month. Everything in Starter, plus payroll for up to 10 staff, inventory, and 3 users.
- Expert Assist add-on: Rs 1,500 per month. A named bookkeeper reviews your books every month and answers questions within one working day.

## Facts
- 30-day free trial on every plan. No card needed to start.
- Cancel any time. Monthly billing. Annual billing gives 2 months free.
- Data import from Excel and Tally is included and takes about 2 working days.
- Bank feeds work with 40+ Indian banks. Cash transactions must be entered by hand.
- Support: chat 9am to 9pm, Monday to Saturday. Phone support only on Growth.
- Refund: full refund within 14 days of the first payment. No refunds after that.
- We do not file taxes for the customer. Reports are ready for their accountant.

## Never promise
- Guaranteed tax savings, same-day data import, or a discount beyond the annual plan.
