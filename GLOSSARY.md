# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Agent | The human sales agent who is practising. |
| Customer | The AI playing a buyer in a scenario. |
| Coach | The AI that judges the agent's replies. Never speaks as the customer. |
| Scenario | One customer to practise with: a public brief the agent sees, and a persona with hidden objections the agent does not. |
| Hidden objection | A concern the customer holds back until the conversation reaches it. |
| Product facts | The only truth about the product. Used by the customer and to check the agent's claims. |
| SQS | Sales Quality Score. Behaviours scored 1 to 5 and combined by weight into a percentage. |
| Behaviour | One line of the SQS rubric, such as Discovery or Empathy. |
| Coach note | The coach's comment on one reply: good, mixed or improve, with a line to try instead. |
| Scorecard | The end-of-call report: SQS, each behaviour with evidence, strengths, growth areas, next drill. |
| Offline mode | No model key: scripted customer and rule-of-thumb scoring. |
| Marker | Anything agents are coached on. Three kinds: rubric, survey, checklist. |
| Survey marker | A question the customer answers in character after the call, such as tNPS or ProSat. |
| Driver | A behaviour that moves a survey marker. |
| Checklist marker | Items marked yes, no or not applicable. A critical item that is missed is called out. |
